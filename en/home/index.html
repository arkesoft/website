BEFORE WE MEET

A few
good questions.

Which digital needs can you help with?+

We work on bespoke software, corporate websites, e-commerce, mobile applications and social media management. We also consider integration, content and maintenance needs within the project scope.

How does a project begin?+

We begin with a conversation about your needs, goals and current situation. We then prepare a tailored scope, timeline and proposal.

Can you improve our existing system?+

Yes. We first review your existing infrastructure, identify what to keep and where improvements are possible. Together, we decide whether to refine or rebuild.

Do you work with brands outside Türkiye?+

We welcome projects from brands in different countries through remote collaboration and regular reviews. Language, time zones and delivery are agreed from the start.
