### prism.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic widescreen hero for a bespoke digital design and software studio. Create a photorealistic optical sculpture: many extremely thin precision-cut smoked glass fins forming a flowing wave, a narrow warm amber light traveling through their edges. Sculpture concentrated center-right; left third mostly dark negative space for large white website typography. Wide 16:9 composition. This evokes connected digital systems and precision craft, not a literal computer. No text, logos, watermark, people, laptops or existing brand objects. Original restrained art direction, gallery-quality.
