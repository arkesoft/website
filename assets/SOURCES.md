### digital-studio.webp

Yerleşik imagegen kullanıldı; çıktı incelendi ve kalite 88 WebP olarak proje klasörüne kaydedildi. Tam üretim istemi:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: wide cinematic hero photograph for bespoke software and digital design agency. Landscape 16:9, luxurious restrained editorial art direction. On the right two-thirds: a very elegant thin unbranded titanium desktop display and generic slim mobile touchscreen on a charcoal stone monolithic desk, photographed from slightly low three-quarter angle. Screen shows a beautifully sophisticated dark ivory and graphite architectural website with a large photograph, side navigation, refined interface blocks and subtle code editor panel visible behind; smartphone shows a refined commerce app. Warm bronze sidelight defines hardware edges, huge black negative space on left 40 percent for a website heading. Background dark modern studio with faint vertical warm architectural slits. Palette charcoal, champagne metal and ivory. Real tactile microtexture, deep blacks, excellent optical realism, luxury brand campaign photography. No recognizable hardware branding or trademark, no Apple or other logo, no watermark, no decorative neon, no floating objects, no collage, no extra readable text. Must immediately communicate exceptionally crafted digital products and software, not a real estate company. Not a website screenshot.

Üretilmiş bir görselin kullanımı konusunda mutlak hukuki hak garantisi verilmez. Yukarıdaki bilgiler teknik kaynak geçmişini belirtir.

### architecture.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ultra-wide cinematic website hero background for an exclusive digital design agency. Create one editorial architectural photograph, landscape 16:9. An extraordinary sculptural modern building in a dark volcanic desert at blue hour, a cantilevered rectangular pavilion of dark bronze metal and glass with a long warm illuminated horizontal slit, monolithic black stone foreground, subtle rugged distant mountains, thin atmospheric mist, real architectural photography shot on a 35mm lens, huge dark negative space in left half and upper sky for white heading overlay. Main architecture occupies right-center. Mood: extremely understated private luxury, refined architectural magazine, meticulous physical materials and natural imperfection, black charcoal, warm bronze, desaturated slate sky. Beautiful dramatic lighting, realistic reflections on slightly wet stone. Not futuristic science fiction, no glowing neon, no abstract floating blobs, no cars, no humans, no text, no logo, no watermark. This is a photograph, not a website screenshot.

### atelier.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium agency portfolio photograph, landscape 3:2. A meticulous studio photograph of three luxury skincare objects on a pale warm travertine block: one dark amber glass perfume bottle with a black geometric cap, one matte ivory cylindrical jar, one slim brushed bronze tube. Minimal small label on perfume says ATELIER, no other readable text. Sculptural late afternoon sun from side, long sharp beautiful shadows on warm off-white backdrop, restrained Celine campaign meets Aesop art direction. Natural real material texture, medium format editorial product photography, sophisticated quiet luxury. Objects occupy center-right and occupy 60 percent frame. No surreal elements, no floating objects, no digital glow, no watermark. Extremely beautiful physical still life, not a web screenshot.

### mobile.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: wide luxury digital agency portfolio image. Photorealistic Apple-quality product photograph of two sophisticated unbranded dark titanium smartphones placed diagonally on a large dark charcoal stone block, near black studio background, side grazing light beautifully defines thin metal edges. Front phone displays an elegant minimal cream hotel booking app with a large photo of a Mediterranean modern villa over sea, tiny title 'CASA' and spare black typography with dates and a bronze action bar. Rear phone shows dark titanium back, no logos. Editorial luxurious campaign image, subtle natural material grain, rich blacks, polished cinematic composition, plenty negative space. Landscape 3:2. No extra objects, no neon, no sci-fi, no gradients behind subjects, no watermark.
